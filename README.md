# 🚀 Flagship Project — MultiDukkan

**MultiDukkan** is a modern multi-tenant ERP/POS platform designed for small and medium businesses to replace paper-based workflows and scattered Excel sheets.

### Current Modules

- 📦 Inventory Management
- 🛒 Sales & Orders
- 📑 Purchases
- 👥 Customers & Suppliers
- 💳 Payments
- 📒 Ledger & Financial Tracking
- 📊 Reporting & Analytics

### Engineering Decisions I'm Proud Of

- Designed the ledger around **append-only accounting principles**, where financial corrections are represented through reversal entries rather than modifying historical records.
- Order totals and payment status are **derived from transactional data** instead of duplicated wherever possible, reducing synchronization issues.
- Centralized financial calculations into a dedicated **LedgerService** to keep business rules consistent across the application.
- Order items snapshot product information and pricing to preserve historical invoices even after products change.
- Performance is treated as a feature—avoiding unnecessary queries and optimizing data access as the application grows.
